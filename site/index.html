Spirit Trainers.

Before behavior, there is spirit

Skill is not enough. Knowledge is not enough. Intelligence is not enough.

Something decides how a person uses all three — under pressure, over time, when it is difficult. Call it the human interior: character, resilience, purpose, judgment, inner strength. Every serious institution already tries to develop it. Almost none of them call it by a single name.

A missing category

We already accept a family of trainers.

  • Athletic Trainersphysical capacity
  • Personal Trainersone-to-one conditioning
  • Corporate Trainersorganizational skill
  • Dog Trainersanimal behavior
  • AI Trainersmachine behavior
  • Spirit Trainersthe human interior — unclaimed

The activity is at least as old as any of them. What is missing is not the work. What is missing is the name — and the small surprise you feel that it doesn't already exist is the opportunity.

The layer beneath the others

The name is either an asset on your balance sheet, or a headline on a competitor's.
