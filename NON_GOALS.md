# NON_GOALS.md

> What this asset is emphatically *not*. Stating non-goals early prevents
> misreading and protects the asset's value: a category name is diminished the
> moment it is mistaken for one of the things below. This document is a hard gate
> — anything that pushes the asset toward a listed non-goal fails review.

---

## SpiritTrainers is not:

- **not** a therapy platform, or any mental-health service,
- **not** a religion, a faith, or a doctrine,
- **not** a spiritual movement, practice, or path,
- **not** a coaching service, a coaching directory, or a coaching brand,
- **not** a certification body or a standards authority,
- **not** a self-help program or a motivation product,
- **not** a personal brand attached to any individual,
- **not** a domain-for-sale listing,
- **not** a claim to have invented a field, industry, or category.

## Why each non-goal matters

Each item below is a specific way the asset could be misread, and what that
misreading would cost.

- **Therapy / mental-health.** Would import clinical claims the asset must never
  make (`CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`) and narrow a broad category to a regulated service.
- **Religion / spirituality.** Would collapse the secular, universal meaning of
  *spirit* (`CANONICAL_MEANING.md`) into a sectarian one, destroying
  cross-cultural reach.
- **Coaching (service / directory / brand).** Would drop the asset from a
  *category above the field* into *one competitor inside it* — the single most
  damaging misread. (See `CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`, Drift A.)
- **Certification / standards body.** Would imply an operating authority the asset
  does not run and cannot warrant. The name *could* extend to a standard someday;
  the asset itself is not one.
- **Self-help / motivation.** Would trade the asset's method register (deliberate,
  trainable capability) for the feeling register the asset explicitly rejects.
- **Personal brand.** Would make the name mortal by tying it to one person; the
  name must be able to outlive anyone.
- **Domain-for-sale listing.** Would cap the valuation at the commodity domain
  market and repel the strategic buyer the asset is built for
  (`ACQUISITION_THESIS.md`).
- **Inventing a field.** Would replace a narrow, defensible claim with an
  over-claim that is easy to dismiss. The asset *names*; it does not *found*.

## The distinction the non-goals protect

Every item above is a way of turning a **name** into a **thing that operates**.
The asset's entire value rests on the opposite posture: it is a category-level
*name and the case for it*, not an operating entity. The non-goals are the fence
around that posture.
