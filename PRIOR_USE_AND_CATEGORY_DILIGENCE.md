## 1. The distinction this document defends

The asset's claim is **not** that the words "Spirit Trainers" have never been
written or spoken. That claim would be false, and one search would prove it
false. The asset's claim is narrower and sturdier:

> **The words exist and have surfaced in scattered, unrelated ways. What has
> never formed is a *consolidated, category-level* identity for developing the
> human interior. That category position is open, and `SpiritTrainers.com` is
> the exact-match name and the case for it.**

Two different things are being separated, and the whole reframe of v0.2 rests on
keeping them apart:

| Phrase existence | Category consolidation |
|------------------|------------------------|
| The words appear here and there. | A market has settled on the words as *the* name. |
| Easy to verify; obviously true. | Has not happened for the human interior. |
| Does **not** create value on its own. | Is exactly the position the asset offers. |

A buyer is not paying for novelty of the phrase. They are paying for the
opportunity to be the party that consolidates it — under the exact-match domain,
with a public thesis already built.

## 2. Register of prior and parallel uses

Each item below is a real, documented use of the phrase (or a near form). None of
them is a consolidated category identity for human development; each is either
abandoned, internal, or from an unrelated domain. Together they are the evidence
*for* the reframe, not against it.

### 2.1 An abandoned U.S. trademark application (2004–2005)
- **Mark:** SPIRIT TRAINERS (standard characters).
- **U.S. serial no.:** 78446793.
- **Filed:** 7 July 2004. **Applicant:** Jeffrey D. Bader (associated with
  William M. Donnelly).
- **Goods/services (Intl. Class 041):** "Yoga and Meditation and wellness
  education and training based on the traditional 8 Limbs of Yoga…"
- **Status:** **Dead / abandoned** as of 14 September 2005 — "abandoned, failure
  to respond or late response." The application never matured into a
  registration.
- **What it shows:** the phrase was reached for once, two decades ago, for a
  *narrow yoga/meditation offering* — not for a category above human
  development — and it lapsed. It demonstrates that the words are natural enough
  to be filed, and that no live federal registration resulted from that filing.
- **What it does not show:** it is **not** a clearance opinion. A dead
  application does not by itself make a mark available; other filings, foreign
  marks, and common-law uses may exist. Clearance is the buyer's own step
  (see §5).

### 2.2 An internal corporate role: the "SIX Spirit Trainer" community
- **User:** SIX, the Swiss financial-market-infrastructure company, runs an
  internal "SIX Spirit" culture programme whose facilitators are called **Spirit
  Trainers** — employees who run values and collaboration workshops for
  colleagues.
- **What it shows:** the exact two-word phrase is *already intuitive to serious
  institutions* as a label for people who develop the human/cultural interior of
  an organization. This is corroboration of the thesis, not a competitor: it is
  an internal HR role inside one company, not a category brand or a domain.
- **What it does not show:** no claim of endorsement, affiliation, or partnership
  with SIX is made or implied (`CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md` §3).

### 2.3 A lexical collision: "Spirit" as footwear (British English)
- **Use:** Hotter, a UK footwear brand, sells a women's lace-up trainer called
  **"Spirit."** In British English, *trainers* means athletic shoes.
- **What it shows:** the compound "Spirit" + "trainers" has a real, everyday
  reading in British English that has **nothing to do with human development** —
  a sneaker. This is a genuine ambiguity the earlier framing ("universal,
  cross-cultural, no ambiguity") did not account for.
- **Why it does not defeat the thesis:** the collision is register- and
  region-specific and is resolved by context. In American English *trainers*
  primarily denotes people who train; even in British English, "Spirit Trainers"
  as a plural agent noun (people) separates cleanly from "Spirit" as a shoe
  model. The asset's job is to *fix the human-development reading* under the
  exact-match `.com` — which is precisely what an owned category position does.
  The collision lowers the claim from "no ambiguity" to "an ambiguity the owner
  is positioned to resolve," which is more honest and still valuable.

## 3. The underlying practice is real and trainable

Separately from the words, the *activity* the name points at is well attested.
Peer-reviewed randomized controlled trials and meta-analyses of workplace and
first-responder **resilience training** — programs that combine character
strengths, cognitive, and emotional components — report measurable effects on
the interior capacities the name describes. The practice is real; what it lacks
is a single name. This is the observation (`OBSERVATION.md`) shown from the
evidence side: the work is funded and studied under many scattered labels
(resilience, grit, character, readiness, mental performance), and no consolidated
category name spans them. (Sources in `SOURCE_REGISTER.md`.)

## 4. What the register changes — and what it does not

**It changes the claim's wording, in three places already updated in v0.2:**

- from "the name does not exist" → "no *consolidated* category name has formed";
- from "the phrase was always available / inevitable" → "the position is open";
- from "universal, no ambiguity" → "a broadly legible name with a known lexical
  collision the owner is positioned to resolve."

**It does not change the asset's core, which is strengthened by the honesty:**

- The human interior is real, trained across many fields, and named
  inconsistently. (Unchanged; now evidenced.)
- No party has consolidated a top-level name for it. (Unchanged; the prior uses
  are scattered, abandoned, internal, or off-domain — none is consolidation.)
- `SpiritTrainers.com` is the exact-match identity and the public case for that
  position. (Unchanged; this is what transfers.)

## 5. Honest limits (the buyer's own diligence)

This document is disclosure, not a warranty, and explicitly **not legal advice.**

- **Trademark clearance is the buyer's step.** Statuses change; other national
  and international filings, and common-law uses, may exist. A buyer intending to
  operate the name should commission a professional clearance search in their
  jurisdictions and classes of interest. The asset transfers a domain and a
  thesis, **not** trademark rights and **not** the exclusive right to the words.
- **Owning the domain is not owning the phrase.** `SpiritTrainers.com` gives its
  holder that exact address, not a monopoly on the two words. The value is
  positional (exact-match identity + published thesis), not proprietary over
  language.
- **Searches are point-in-time.** The uses catalogued here were verified on the
  date in `SOURCE_REGISTER.md`. New uses can appear. This file should be
  re-checked before any transaction closes.
- **Absence of evidence is not proof of absence.** "No consolidated category
  name" is a judgment from public search, not a measured certainty. It is
  defensible, not provable.
