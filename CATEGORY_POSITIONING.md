# CATEGORY_POSITIONING.md

> Where the category sits in the wider world, and why one name can span many
> markets. This is the "map" document — it connects the name to the economy it
> describes without pretending to measure it with false precision.

---

## 1. Positioning statement

> **Spirit Trainers** names the layer *beneath* skills, knowledge, and
> intelligence — the layer that decides how those are used. It is not a
> sub-category of coaching, wellness, or education. It is a name that sits
> *above* all of them, because each of those fields is already, quietly, doing
> spirit-training under a different label.

## 2. The layer model

Most human-development markets operate on the top three layers of a person:

```
   ┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
   │  SKILL      — what you can do        │
   ├─────────────────────────────────────┤
   │  KNOWLEDGE  — what you know          │
   ├─────────────────────────────────────┤
   │  INTELLIGENCE — how fast you process │
   ├═════════════════════════════════════┤
   │  SPIRIT     — how you use all of it  │  ← the unnamed layer
   └─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Skill, knowledge, and intelligence each have mature markets, professions, and
category names. The bottom layer — the one that governs the other three under
pressure — has practitioners everywhere but **no consolidated top-level name.**
That is the position "Spirit Trainers" claims.

## 3. One name, many markets

The category is broad *by design*, because the interior it names is the same
interior every serious institution is already trying to develop. The name can be
worn by:

- **Executive & leadership coaching** — judgment and composure at the top.
- **Corporate wellbeing & resilience** — sustaining people through pressure.
- **Military & first-responder readiness** — performance when it is hardest.
- **Education & youth development** — character before credentials.
- **Sports psychology & mental performance** — the inner game.
- **Rehabilitation & recovery** — rebuilding the will to continue.
- **Higher education** — institutes for leadership and human development.

The strategic point is not that the category is *large*. It is that the category
is **singular across many industries**: one name, many markets. A buyer in any
one of these sectors can see their own work reflected in the name — which is
exactly what makes the name valuable to *all* of them.

## 4. Why breadth is a feature, not a weakness

A narrow name (e.g. "GritCoachHQ") is legible but small: it fits one niche and
dies with it. A broad-but-vague name ("Human Potential Inc.") is large but
forgettable: it fits everywhere and means nothing.

"Spirit Trainers" is the rare middle: **broad in reach, specific in meaning.** It
spans many markets *and* says something exact (see `CANONICAL_MEANING.md`). That
combination is what lets a single owner position it as the umbrella under which a
whole class of organizations could eventually sit.

## 5. Positioning guardrails

- Do **not** quantify the market with invented figures. The asset's credibility
  depends on never fabricating a "$X billion" number. Reference the *sectors*,
  not fake totals. (See `governance/QUALITY_GATE.md`.)
- Do **not** position the name as a competitor to any one sector. It is an
  umbrella, not a rival.
- Do **not** let "broad" become "generic." Every time breadth is invoked, the
  fixed meaning must be invoked with it.

## 6. The one-line positioning for external use

> **Spirit Trainers — the category name for developing the human interior:
> resilience, character, and judgment, as a trainable discipline, across every
> institution that already does it under another name.**
