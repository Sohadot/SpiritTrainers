# CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md

> The single most important guardrail in the repository. It defines exactly what
> this asset claims and — more importantly — what it must **never** claim. Most
> ways this asset could fail are ways it could over-claim. This file exists to
> make those failures impossible to commit accidentally.

---

## 1. The claim, stated minimally

> **The activity of training the human spirit exists and is ancient. A clean,
> canonical, top-level *name* for it does not. "Spirit Trainers" is proposed as
> that name.**

That is the entire claim. It is deliberately narrow, because a narrow claim is a
defensible claim.

## 2. What the asset MAY claim

- That the activity (developing resilience, character, judgment, inner strength)
  is real and long-standing.
- That it is currently **fragmented** across many vocabularies and fields.
- That no consolidated top-level name has been established for it.
- That "Spirit Trainers" is linguistically well-suited to be that name.
- That owning the name is a *strategic position*.

## 3. What the asset must NEVER claim

- ❌ That it **invented** human development, coaching, or resilience training.
- ❌ That it is a **coaching service, platform, or program.**
- ❌ Any **medical, therapeutic, or clinical** benefit or outcome.
- ❌ Any **religious, spiritual, or metaphysical** truth or authority.
- ❌ **Revenue, traffic, valuation, or market-size figures** stated as fact.
- ❌ That the buyer will **succeed** by acquiring the name (it sells a position,
  not a result).
- ❌ Endorsement, affiliation, or partnership with any named person or
  institution that has not explicitly agreed to it.

## 4. The two drifts (and how to catch them)

Nearly every over-claim is one of two drifts. Reviewers and writers should scan
for both before anything ships.

### Drift A — toward coaching
**Symptom:** language that offers a service, a transformation, or a benefit to a
*person*. Words like "our program," "get started," "your journey," "we help
you."
**Correction:** re-anchor to the *name and category*. The asset addresses a
*buyer of a category*, never a *client of a service*.

### Drift B — toward spirituality
**Symptom:** "spirit" used in a religious, mystical, or metaphysical sense.
Imagery or language of the soul, the sacred, energy, enlightenment.
**Correction:** re-anchor to `CANONICAL_MEANING.md` — "spirit" means human inner
character, resilience, purpose, and moral strength. Secular. Universal.

## 5. The tone boundary

Even where a claim is *permitted*, it must be made in the asset's register:
observational, not promotional. The asset **notices** that the name is available
and inevitable; it does not **hype** it. Hype is itself a form of over-claiming —
it claims certainty the asset cannot honestly assert.

## 6. Why the boundary protects value

Counter-intuitively, the **narrower** the claim, the **higher** the value:

- A precise, self-limiting thesis is credible, and credibility is what makes a
  strategic buyer pay a strategic price.
- An over-claiming thesis ("the future of coaching!") is indistinguishable from a
  thousand marketing pages and prices like one.

Discipline in what we refuse to claim is not caution. It is the pricing strategy.

## 7. Enforcement

This file is a hard gate in `governance/QUALITY_GATE.md`. No content — document,
journal article, or site copy — passes review if it violates section 3. Every
exception (there should be none) must be argued and recorded in
`DECISION_LOG.md`.
