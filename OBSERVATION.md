# OBSERVATION.md

> Why this asset exists at all. Before a thesis there is an observation — a plain
> fact about the world that the thesis then interprets. This document states the
> observation and nothing more. It makes no argument and asks for no decision;
> it only points at something already true.

---

## The observation

> **There is a cross-disciplinary human practice — developing the interior that
> governs how people act under pressure — that is carried out, seriously and at
> scale, by many fields at once, and yet has no single canonical name at the
> category level.**

That is the whole observation. It is not a claim about the future, a prediction,
or a proposal. It is a description of a present condition.

## Three things the observation is careful to be

1. **Descriptive, not prescriptive.** It reports what is, not what should be. The
   thesis (in `CATEGORY_THESIS.md`) is where interpretation and proposal begin.
   The observation must stay clean of both, or the whole structure inherits a
   bias at its root.
2. **About a name, not an activity.** The observation does *not* say the activity
   is missing, undervalued, or misunderstood. The activity is abundant. What is
   absent is a *canonical category name* for it. Keeping this distinction exact is
   the discipline the entire asset depends on.
3. **Cross-disciplinary by nature.** The practice does not belong to one field.
   It appears in education, the military, sport, organizational life, and more.
   The observation is precisely that it is *distributed* — and that distribution
   is why no single field has produced the top-level name.

## What makes the observation credible

An observation is credible when a reasonable person, shown the evidence, would
say "yes, that is simply the case." The evidence is ordinary and public:

- We readily accept *Athletic Trainers*, *Personal Trainers*, *Corporate
  Trainers*, *Dog Trainers*, and, recently, *AI Trainers* as category names.
- Each names a domain of deliberate capability-building.
- The domain of the human interior is at least as large and as old — and yet the
  parallel top-level name is not in common use.

The gap is not hidden. It is hiding in plain sight, which is the usual condition
of things that are true but unnamed. (The specifics are catalogued in
`CATEGORY_EVIDENCE.md`.)

## What the observation does *not* yet claim

- It does not claim that "Spirit Trainers" is the right name. (That is the
  thesis.)
- It does not claim the gap is an opportunity. (That is the implication.)
- It does not claim anyone should acquire anything. (That is the acquisition.)

The observation earns the right to those later moves only by being, first, simply
and defensibly true.

## Position in the argument

```
OBSERVATION  →  THESIS  →  CATEGORY  →  IMPLICATIONS  →  ACQUISITION
   (here)
```

Everything downstream is an interpretation of this one fact. If the observation
is wrong, the asset is wrong. It is stated first, and alone, so that it can be
examined on its own terms before any argument is built on top of it.
