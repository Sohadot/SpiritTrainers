# PROJECT_DOCTRINE.md

> The operating philosophy of the asset. Governance documents describe *how* the
> asset is maintained; this one describes the *spirit* in which every decision
> is made. When a rule is silent, decide in the direction this doctrine points.

---

## 1. Thesis before interface, always

Work proceeds in one order and never the reverse:

> **Thesis → Structure → Interface.**

The governing documents are written first because they decide what the interface
is allowed to say. A pixel is never placed before its meaning is fixed. This is
why the site was built *after* the thesis, not before it.

## 2. The asset is a document, not a product

This is a **sovereign category asset**. Its value is in meaning, not operation.
Every choice is evaluated against one question:

> *Does this make a strategic buyer more likely to conclude that "Spirit
> Trainers" is a category worth owning?*

If a proposed feature, page, or sentence does not serve that question, it does
not belong — no matter how well-made it is.

## 3. Subtraction is the default

The asset competes by removing what everyone else adds. No stock imagery, no
motivational copy, no urgency mechanics, no feature bloat. When in doubt, cut.
The strongest version of this asset is the one with the least in it that still
makes the case.

## 4. Precision over persuasion

The asset does not hype. It *notices*. It states narrow, defensible claims and
lets the reader draw the conclusion. Persuasion that outruns what can be honestly
claimed is treated as a defect (see `CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`), not a flourish.

## 5. The name outlives everyone

No individual's face, voice, or authority is attached to the asset. It is built
to be inherited. Any decision that makes the asset depend on one person is
suspect by default.

## 6. Honesty is a pricing strategy

The asset states its own limits plainly: it does not claim revenue, does not
guarantee buyer outcomes, does not pretend the category is already established.
This is not modesty — it is what makes the thesis credible, and credibility is
what commands a strategic price.

## 7. Every material choice is logged

The asset must be explicable years later, to an owner who was not present when it
was built. Material decisions are recorded in `DECISION_LOG.md` with their
rationale and rejected alternatives. An unlogged decision is treated as
provisional.

## 8. Consistency is enforced, not hoped for

The meaning of the name (`CANONICAL_MEANING.md`) and the limits of the claim
(`CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md`) are hard gates in `QUALITY_GATE.md`. Consistency across
dozens of documents and pages is a property the process guarantees, not a thing
individual authors are trusted to remember.

## 9. Reversibility

Prefer changes that can be undone. The asset's history (git + the decision log)
should always allow a return to any prior coherent state. Irreversible moves —
especially anything touching the canonical name or its meaning — require the
heaviest scrutiny in `CHANGE_CONTROL.md`.
